Dairy Queen operates restaurants in many different markets, and menu prices are not necessarily identical at every location. Franchise pricing, local costs, promotions, taxes, and ordering channels can all affect the final amount customers pay.

For that reason, historical price information is best used as a general reference. Customers should check their local restaurant for the most accurate current price.
